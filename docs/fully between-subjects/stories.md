# Stories and Features

The present study includes three vignettes with three versions each (i.e., 9 total), whereby each participant completes all three stories but sees only ONE version of each story.
All stories are adapted from Wu et al. (2026).


In the tables below, the colum called "**Compared to original**" compares each story feature against the original story's features as follows:
- **same**: story feature unchanged
- **renamed**: same story feature, but a character's name changed
- **manipulated**: this story feature is varied/manipulated across its versions
- **changed**: other changes made to the story feature in question
- **omitted**: feature was included in the original story but left out in this story's version

## Blake (originally Nadia; varies gender)

### Blake_female (matches the original story)

Blake is a 34-year-old White woman who works as a software engineer. She has red hair and is a timid but organized person. She enjoys collecting vinyl records and oil painting.

Blake and her husband Cameron have been married for five years. Two years ago, Cameron spent four months in jail for physically abusing Blake. After that, they started going to couples therapy and for a while their relationship improved.

Recently, however, Cameron has been acting more violent towards Blake when drunk, and on one occasion Blake went to the emergency room for bruises she suffered. One night, Cameron came home in an extremely intoxicated state and began screaming at Blake that he was going to kill Blake someday. Blake became very scared and ran into their bedroom to hide. Cameron followed Blake and started hitting her. Out of panic, Blake grabbed the loaded, licensed revolver that Cameron kept under the bed and shot Cameron. Cameron died in the hospital three hours later.

*Story changes from the original:* Names changed (Nadia → Blake; Franklin → Cameron). In places, pronouns were replaced with names for clarity (e.g., "kill her" → "kill Blake").

## Jamie (originally Qian; varies race)

### Jamie_asian (matches the original story)

Jamie is a 27-year-old Asian male waiter in a small town. He is hard-working and has a humorous personality, but can be stubborn sometimes. He enjoys watching sports, listening to jazz music, and does not smoke.

One Friday night, Jamie was hanging out at the local bar with some cousins who were visiting from out of town. They had several rounds of drinks over the course of two hours. Wilson, a car mechanic who lives and works in the same small town, was also at the bar that night and had been there for about an hour. He approached Jamie's party and began saying racial slurs to them.

Jamie was angered by Wilson's comments, but did not talk back to Wilson at first. Wilson then playfully punched Jamie while continuing to make derogatory comments. Jamie suddenly got up and violently pushed Wilson, knocking him to the ground and causing him serious injury.

*Story changes from the original:* Name changed (Qian → Jamie). New sentence: "Jamie was angered by Wilson's comments, but did not talk back to Wilson at first," so features 13 and 14 are now described in the story. The punch sentence now names Wilson, and the story is split into three paragraphs.

## Jordan (originally Chris; varies age)

### Jordan_10 (matches the original story)

Jordan is a 10-year-old White boy who is about 5 feet tall and has an average IQ. He has a curious personality, enjoys playing hide-and-seek as well as swimming, and is a picky eater.

Jordan admires his father a lot. His father is an avid recreational deer hunter who owns several hunting guns. All of the guns are kept in a locked cabinet, and Jordan's father usually keeps the key on himself.

One day, Jordan was playing hide-and-seek with friends at home and hid in the hall closet. There, he found the key to the gun cabinet in his father's jacket, which was hanging in the closet. Jordan curiously opened the cabinet and began playing with one of the pistols. As he was holding the pistol, he accidentally discharged it and shot one of his friends in the shoulder. The friend was taken to the hospital for treatment of severe wounds.

*Story changes from the original:* Name changed (Chris → Jordan). Weight replaced with height ("weighs 70 lbs" → "is about 5 feet tall").
